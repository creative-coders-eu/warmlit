Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

For residents of Washington, Nevada and other US states with consumer health data laws. In short: the Warmlit app doesn’t collect consumer health data. We receive it only when you email us or take part in our beta, we never sell it, and we share it only with Apple, if we invite you to our beta by email.

Effective October 31, 2026Version 1.1

This policy is published by Creative Coders sp. z o.o. (“we”, “us”), the maker of Warmlit. It covers the Warmlit app for iPhone, this website, emails you send us and beta testing through Apple’s TestFlight. Our Privacy Policy covers everything else.

Consumer health data we collect, and how we use it

  • Meal photos, only with your consent. Meal photo estimates (Warmlit Pro, optional): when you choose to analyse a photo of your food, the photo and proof of your Pro purchase go to our server, which sends the photo to Anthropic to estimate calories, protein, carbs and fiber. We don’t store the photo or the estimate; Anthropic may keep the request for up to 30 days for safety monitoring and doesn’t use it to train its models. We ask for your consent in the app before the first photo, and you can withdraw it in Warmlit’s settings at any time. We don’t sell this data or use it for anything else.
  • Through the app: nothing else. What you log in Warmlit, such as your medicines, doses, injection sites, side effects, weight, protein and water, is stored on your iPhone and used there only to show you your log, reminders and charts. Warmlit has no account and no servers, and it never sends what you log to us or to anyone else. We can’t see it.
  • Through beta testing. If you test a beta version of Warmlit through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple passes us the crash reports and usage information that TestFlight collects automatically, and the comments and screenshots you send as feedback, which may show health information, such as a medicine, a dose or a symptom. If we invite you to the beta because you take a GLP-1 medicine, our tester list (your name, your email address and whether you use a pill or a shot or are switching from another app) is consumer health data too, and so are the name and email address we give Apple if we invite you by email. We use all of this only to run the beta and fix problems, and we delete our copies within 12 months after the beta ends.
  • By email: only what you choose to send. If you write to us and include health information, such as a medicine, a dose or a symptom, we ask before we use it, and then use it only to answer your message. Please leave it out; we can help without it. We delete support emails 24 months after your last message.

Where it comes from

From you, when you include health information in an email to us or tell us whether you use a pill or a shot (or are switching from another app) so we can invite you to the beta; and from Apple’s TestFlight, which passes us your beta feedback and the crash reports and usage information it collects.

Consumer health data we share, and with whom

We never sell consumer health data, and we share it in one case only: if we invite you to the beta by email because you take a GLP-1 medicine, we give Apple Inc., which runs TestFlight, your name and email address so that it can send you the invitation. We do that only because it’s necessary to give you the beta you asked to join. No affiliate and no other third party receives consumer health data from us.

Our email provider, Zoho Corporation B.V., stores our mailbox for us as our processor and handles it only on our instructions. Feedback you send through TestFlight goes to Apple, which keeps it for a year under its own privacy policy and passes it to us. Our tester list stays with us.

If you choose to, Warmlit can save weight, protein and water to the Health app on your iPhone, and you can export files and send them wherever you like. Those things happen on your iPhone, at your direction, and nothing is sent to us.

Tracking across websites and apps

Apart from the security logs that GitHub, our website host, keeps of visits to this website, and the statistics Apple collects if you choose to share analytics with app developers, no third party collects consumer health data or other personal information about you over time and across websites or apps through Warmlit or this website. Warmlit contains no third-party code, and this website has no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data;
  • give you access to it, including a list of any third parties and affiliates we shared it with (at most Apple, if we invited you to the beta by email);
  • delete it;
  • withdraw your consent, or stop collecting or sharing it.

Email admin@ccoders.eu. Requests are free. We answer within 45 days. If we need longer, we’ll tell you why within those 45 days and take no more than 45 days more. To check that a request comes from you, we may ask you to write from the email address you used before. We’ll never ask for health details.

What you log in Warmlit never reaches us, so you control it in the app: you can view, export and delete it at any time, or erase everything Warmlit stores on your iPhone, including its automatic copies, with Delete all data in Warmlit’s settings.

Appeals

If we decline your request, reply to our answer with the word “Appeal”. We’ll respond in writing within 45 days and explain what we did or didn’t do, and why. If we deny your appeal, you can complain to your state attorney general, for example in Washington at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint or in Nevada at ag.nv.gov.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects how we handle consumer health data, we’ll also say so in Warmlit’s release notes before it takes effect. Before we collect, use or share consumer health data in a new way, we’ll tell you and ask for your consent.
