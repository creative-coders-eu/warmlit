Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Purchases belong to your Apple Account, so there’s nothing to sign in to in Warmlit.

  1. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy Warmlit Pro.
  2. In Warmlit, open the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases. You’ll also find it on the screen where you choose a Warmlit Pro plan.

Still not working? Email us with the plan you bought and roughly when. We don’t need receipts or health details.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Apple manages subscriptions. On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Warmlit Pro. From there you can switch between yearly and monthly, or tap Cancel Subscription. You can also get there from the App Store app (tap your profile picture, then Subscriptions), or with Manage subscription in Warmlit’s settings.

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal or trial end date to avoid being charged.
  • You keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
  • Deleting Warmlit doesn’t cancel a subscription.
  • If you buy Lifetime while subscribed, cancel the subscription yourself.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose “Request a refund” and select Warmlit Pro. Apple decides on refund requests; we can’t issue refunds or see your payment details.

How do I export or import my data?

Export is free. In Warmlit’s settings, under Your data, tap:

  • Export CSV: a spreadsheet of your doses, side effects and measurements that opens in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Export backup: a complete copy of your Warmlit data that you can bring back later with Restore from backup.

Warmlit hands the file to the iOS share sheet, so you choose where it goes: Files, AirDrop, email or anywhere else. These files contain your health log, so keep them somewhere safe. With Warmlit Pro you can also create a PDF report for your doctor.

Switching from another app? Tap Import CSV in the same place to bring your history with you. The file is read on your iPhone and isn’t uploaded.

How do I move Warmlit to a new iPhone?

Warmlit’s data is part of your iPhone backup, so it moves to a new iPhone when you restore from an iCloud or computer backup, or when you transfer directly from your old iPhone. For extra peace of mind, export a backup file first.

Warmlit doesn’t sync between devices: if you use two iPhones, each keeps its own log. Warmlit Pro comes with your Apple Account; if it doesn’t show up on the new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases.

How does Face ID lock work?

Turn on Face ID lock in Warmlit’s settings, under Privacy & security. From then on, Warmlit asks for Face ID before it shows your log. The first time, iOS asks for permission to use Face ID; you can change that later in the iPhone Settings app under Apps → Warmlit.

Face ID is handled by iOS: Warmlit never sees your face data. The lock covers the app itself. Reminders, the Live Activity and widgets don’t show your medicine’s name unless you turn on Show medicine names in Warmlit’s settings, and you can also hide notification previews under Settings → Notifications.

Why doesn’t Warmlit have an account?

Accounts mean servers, and servers mean your health log sitting in a database somewhere. We’d rather not hold it at all. Your entries are stored on your iPhone, Apple handles purchases, and there’s nothing to sign up for or forget.

The trade-off: we can’t recover your data for you, and Warmlit doesn’t sync between devices. Use Export or your iPhone backup to keep a copy.

How does Apple Health sync work?

Apple Health sync is free. In Warmlit’s settings, tap Apple Health, turn on Sync with Apple Health, and choose what to share on Apple’s permission screen.

  • Warmlit reads and writes weight, protein and water, so an entry made in either app shows up in both. You can turn each of the three on or off.
  • That’s all it syncs: Warmlit doesn’t read your medications from the Health app and doesn’t save side effects there.
  • Everything works without Apple Health, too: you can enter weight, protein and water by hand.

To change permissions later, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Warmlit.

How do I delete all my data?

In Warmlit’s settings, under Your data, tap Delete all data to erase everything Warmlit has stored on your iPhone, including its automatic backup copies. If you used Apple Health sync, you can choose to delete the entries Warmlit saved to Apple Health at the same time. This can’t be undone, so export a backup first if you might want it later. Your Warmlit Pro purchase isn’t affected. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.

Other copies aren’t affected: entries Warmlit saved to Apple Health stay there unless you chose to delete them too (you can also delete them in the Health app), files you exported stay where you saved them, and older iPhone backups may still contain Warmlit data until they’re replaced. We don’t have a copy, so there’s nothing to delete on our side, unless you emailed us; if so, ask us to delete your emails.

What is the pill window?

Some GLP-1 tablets come with a waiting period. For example, oral semaglutide tablets are taken on an empty stomach, followed by a wait of at least 30 minutes before food, drinks or other medicines. Warmlit calls this the pill window.

When you log a tablet that needs a wait, Warmlit starts a countdown on your Lock Screen and, on iPhones that have it, in the Dynamic Island. The countdown uses the waiting time from your medicine’s labeling, and Warmlit shows where that instruction comes from. Instructions differ between countries and products, so if your prescriber told you something different, follow them.

Tablets that can be taken with or without food, such as orforglipron, don’t start a countdown, and weekly shots don’t need one. The countdown is free. On iOS 26 or later you can also turn on Alarm when the window ends in Warmlit’s settings, under Pill window: a free alarm that sounds when the window ends, even in Silent mode.

Why didn’t my reminder arrive?

  • Check that Dose reminders is on in Warmlit’s settings.
  • Check that notifications are on for Warmlit in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications → Warmlit.
  • Check that a Focus isn’t silencing Warmlit.
  • If you use Scheduled Summary, set Warmlit to Immediate Delivery so reminders aren’t held for the summary.
  • Check the reminder time and days on the medication in Warmlit.
  • In Warmlit’s settings, under Medications, check that the medicine isn’t paused and that its own Reminders switch is on. Medicines added by a CSV import start out paused until you set their schedule.
  • Open Warmlit every week or two. It schedules reminders up to 14 days ahead and adds more each time you open it. If you see “Keep your reminders coming”, open the app.

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, so they work offline. Even so, please don’t rely on Warmlit as your only reminder.

Why do brand names appear only in the medication picker?

Medicine brand names are trademarks of the companies that make them. Warmlit is an independent app, not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any drug maker. So we show brand names only in the medication picker, in plain text, to help you recognise the medicine you were prescribed.

Everywhere else, including this website and the App Store, we use generic names such as semaglutide or tirzepatide, and the form, such as daily tablet or weekly shot.

Can Warmlit tell me what dose to take?

No. Warmlit never calculates, recommends or changes a dose, and it won’t suggest when to move to a new dose. You enter the dose and schedule your prescriber gave you. If you’ve missed a dose or aren’t sure what to do, follow your Medication Guide or leaflet and ask your doctor or pharmacist. See the medical disclaimer.
