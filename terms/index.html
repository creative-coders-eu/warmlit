2. Health information

Warmlit is a personal logbook and reminder tool. It is not a regulated medical device, and it doesn’t diagnose, monitor or treat any condition or give medical advice. It never calculates, recommends or changes a dose.

Always take your medicine exactly as prescribed, follow the Medication Guide or patient leaflet that came with it, and check with your doctor or pharmacist before making decisions about your treatment. Reminders and timers depend on your iPhone’s settings and can be delayed or missed, so don’t rely on Warmlit as your only reminder. Please read the full Medical disclaimer.

3. Who Warmlit is for

Warmlit is designed for adults who have been prescribed a GLP-1 or GIP/GLP-1 medicine. It isn’t directed to children: you must be 18 or older to use it. Warmlit needs an iPhone with iOS 18 or later. 6. Warmlit Pro

Warmlit Pro is optional and adds features on top of the free app. Today it includes:

  • Insights. Weight trend by dose step, side effects by days since your dose or by dose step, adherence and your pill-window record.
  • Meal photo estimates. Rough estimates of calories, protein, carbs and fiber from a photo of your food, which you review before saving. Limits: 3 photos during a free trial, then 5 a day and 60 a calendar month. Estimates can be wrong and aren’t medical or nutrition advice.
  • Doctor report. A PDF of your doses, sites, side effects and measurements to share with your healthcare professional.
  • Weekly recap. A short summary of what you logged, written on your iPhone by on-device AI.
  • More widgets. Lock Screen, interactive and StandBy widgets.
  • Alternate app icons and themes.

9. Cancelling

  • You can cancel at any time. On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Warmlit Pro, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also open Subscriptions from your profile in the App Store app.

  • When Pro ends, everything on the free-forever list keeps working, your entries stay on your iPhone, and your dose reminders carry on.
  • If you buy Lifetime while you have a subscription, cancel the subscription yourself. Apple doesn’t cancel it automatically.
