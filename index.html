Why Warmlit

Four promises, built in from day one

Plenty of apps can count down 30 minutes. Warmlit is built around what matters when you take a GLP-1 medicine every morning or every week.

Pill-first, done right

Medicine profiles follow the label: the 30-minute wait after an oral semaglutide tablet, tablets you can take any time with no wait, and weekly shots on your day.

  • Log with one tap from Control Center, a Lock Screen control, the Action button or Siri.
  • The countdown runs on your Lock Screen and, on iPhones that have it, in the Dynamic Island.
  • On iOS 26 or later, a free alarm can sound at the end, even in Silent mode.

Our privacy promise

Your health log is yours. We never see it.

Warmlit has no servers and no accounts, so there’s nothing you log for us to collect, sell or lose. Your entries are stored on your iPhone, and Warmlit never sends them to us or to anyone else.

That’s also why we can’t recover your data for you. Keep your own copy with Export or your iPhone backup.

How the free trial works

  1. Today Full access to Warmlit Pro, free for 7 days.
  2. Day 5 We remind you that your trial ends in 2 days, with a notification if they’re on and in the app either way.
  3. Day 7 You’re billed $39.99 for the year, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before.

Warmlit is offered only on the US App Store, and prices are in US dollars, plus any sales tax that applies. Warmlit always shows the current price before you buy.

Subscriptions are charged to your Apple Account and renew automatically at the same price and length unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Cancel any time in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. The free trial is for new subscribers to the yearly plan. There are no exit offers and no hidden switches. See the Terms of Use.

Warmlit doesn’t sync between devices. Pro unlocks on any iPhone signed in with your Apple Account, but your entries stay on the iPhone where you made them. Move them with Export or your iPhone backup.

* The AI weekly recap needs iOS 26 or later on an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in a supported language and region. Other iPhones get a simpler template recap.

Questions

Good to know

Does Warmlit tell me what dose to take?

No. Warmlit never calculates, recommends or changes a dose. You enter the dose and schedule your prescriber gave you, and Warmlit helps you keep to it.

Why is there no account?

Because we’d rather not hold your health information at all. Your entries are stored on your iPhone, so there’s nothing to sign in to and no copy of your log on our side to leak.

What if I never buy Pro?

You keep everything on the free-forever list. Logging, reminders, the pill-window timer and its alarm, site rotation, side effects, Apple Health sync, your full history and export never move behind the paywall.

What happens when I change phones?

Your entries move with your iPhone backup or when you transfer to a new iPhone. You can also export a backup file or CSV at any time and keep your own copy.

What Warmlit is, and what it isn’t

Warmlit is a personal record-keeping and reminder app for adults who have been prescribed a GLP-1 medicine. It lets you record the doses you take, set reminders for the schedule your prescriber gave you, time the waiting period printed in your medicine’s instructions, note injection sites and side effects, see your own weight, protein and water entries, and export a report to share with your healthcare professional. Warmlit does not diagnose, monitor or treat any disease or condition, does not calculate, recommend or change doses, and does not interpret symptoms or measurements.

Warmlit is not a regulated medical device and doesn’t give medical advice. Always follow your prescriber and the Medication Guide or leaflet that came with your medicine. Read the medical disclaimer.
